## 1. The World Before the True Sun（世界還沒有真正的太陽）

**插圖**：`book_front_01`

> In the beginning, all was silent and still. There was only the empty sky, only the calm sea.
>
> The makers, Sovereign and Quetzal Serpent, glowed in the water. Heart of Sky, Huracan, came down from above. They talked together; they thought together. They said, "Earth," and the earth rose up like a cloud, like a mist.
>
> But the dawn had not yet come. Only a faint light lay over sky and earth. Everything could be seen, yet nothing cast a shadow. The true sun had not yet risen.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| In the beginning, all was silent and still. | 最初，一切都寂靜無聲。 |
| There was only the empty sky, only the calm sea. | 只有空空的天空，只有平靜的海。 |
| The makers, Sovereign and Quetzal Serpent, glowed in the water. | 造物者主宰與綠咬鵑之蛇，在水中發著光。 |
| Heart of Sky, Huracan, came down from above. | 天心烏拉坎從上方降臨。 |
| They talked together; they thought together. | 他們一起商量，一起思索。 |
| They said, "Earth," and the earth rose up like a cloud, like a mist. | 他們說：「大地。」大地就像雲、像霧一樣升起。 |
| But the dawn had not yet come. | 可是，黎明還沒有來。 |
| Only a faint light lay over sky and earth. | 天地間只有微微的光。 |
| Everything could be seen, yet nothing cast a shadow. | 萬物都看得見，卻沒有一樣東西投下影子。 |
| The true sun had not yet risen. | 真正的太陽還沒有升起。 |

**與繁中的差異**：「從天上來」寫成「came down from above」（降臨），英文比較自然；「大地出現了」寫成「rose up」（升起），呼應原典「如雲霧升起」（註 68）。
**保留的原典對句**：like a cloud, like a mist（p.59）。

## 2. The Earth（大地）

**插圖**：`book_front_02`

> Now the earth had mountains, it had forests, and it had homes.
>
> At the edge of a forest stood a house where a family lived. Not far from the house was a ballcourt, and beside the ballcourt a road ran down, down, deep beneath the earth.
>
> The earth lay above; the underworld lay below. This story happened between the two.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| Now the earth had mountains, it had forests, and it had homes. | 大地上有了山，有了森林，也有了人家。 |
| At the edge of a forest stood a house where a family lived. | 森林邊有一間屋子，住著一家人。 |
| Not far from the house was a ballcourt, and beside the ballcourt a road ran down, down, deep beneath the earth. | 離屋子不遠有一座球場；球場旁有一條路，一直往下、往下，通到大地深處。 |
| The earth lay above; the underworld lay below. | 大地在上，地底在下。 |
| This story happened between the two. | 這個故事，就發生在兩者之間。 |

**與繁中的差異**：「一直往下通到地底」用「down, down」重複表現「一直」；「地底」譯成 the underworld，讓第 3 張的 Xibalba 接得上。

## 3. Xibalba Below（地底的西巴爾巴）

**插圖**：`book_front_03`

> Beneath the earth lay Xibalba, the Place of Fear.
>
> There lived the lords of Xibalba. At their head were One Death and Seven Death. Each of the other lords ruled over a sickness: some made people's blood sick, some made them swell, some wasted them down to bone, and some struck them down on the road.
>
> They sat in the darkness under the earth, and what they cared about most was whether others showed them respect.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| Beneath the earth lay Xibalba, the Place of Fear. | 大地底下，是西巴爾巴——恐懼之地。 |
| There lived the lords of Xibalba. | 那裡住著西巴爾巴的諸王。 |
| At their head were One Death and Seven Death. | 為首的是一死神與七死神。 |
| Each of the other lords ruled over a sickness: | 其他的王各掌一種病痛： |
| some made people's blood sick, some made them swell, | 有的讓人的血生病，有的讓人腫脹， |
| some wasted them down to bone, and some struck them down on the road. | 有的讓人消瘦到只剩骨頭，有的讓人倒在路上。 |
| They sat in the darkness under the earth, | 他們坐在地底的黑暗裡， |
| and what they cared about most was whether others showed them respect. | 最在意的，就是別人敬不敬重他們。 |

**與繁中的差異**：無。「the Place of Fear」是 Christenson 對 Xib'alb'a 的意譯（註 231）。

## 4. One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu（一胡納普與七胡納普）

**插圖**：`book_front_04`

> In the darkness, in the night, Grandfather and Grandmother had two sons: One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu.
>
> The elder, One Hunahpu, took a wife. The younger, Seven Hunahpu, never married. He stayed always at One Hunahpu's side, his brother's companion and helper.
>
> Both brothers were great sages, full of knowledge, and they could see far and clearly across the face of the earth. They were good by nature, good from birth.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| In the darkness, in the night, Grandfather and Grandmother had two sons: One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu. | 在黑暗中、在夜裡，祖父與祖母生下了兩個兒子：一胡納普與七胡納普。 |
| The elder, One Hunahpu, took a wife. | 哥哥一胡納普娶了妻子。 |
| The younger, Seven Hunahpu, never married. | 弟弟七胡納普沒有成家。 |
| He stayed always at One Hunahpu's side, his brother's companion and helper. | 他總是陪在一胡納普身邊，是哥哥的同伴與幫手。 |
| Both brothers were great sages, full of knowledge, | 兩兄弟都是了不起的智者，學問很大， |
| and they could see far and clearly across the face of the earth. | 在大地上看得又遠又清楚。 |
| They were good by nature, good from birth. | 他們天性善良，生來善良。 |

**與繁中的差異**：最後一句用「good by nature, good from birth」的對句，比繁中「生來就是善良的人」多一層重複，貼近原典「good by their nature, and in their birth as well」（p.100）。
**保留的原典對句**：in the darkness, in the night（p.99）；the face of the earth（原典慣用語）。

## 5. Grandmother's Family（祖母一家）

**插圖**：`book_front_05`

> One Hunahpu's wife was named Xbaquiyalo. They had two sons. The elder was One Batz, and the younger was One Chouen.
>
> The two brothers passed all their gifts on to the boys. One Batz and One Chouen grew up to be flute players and singers, writers and carvers, jade cutters and workers of gold and silver.
>
> Grandmother watched her grandsons grow. But in time, Xbaquiyalo passed away.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| One Hunahpu's wife was named Xbaquiyalo. | 一胡納普的妻子名叫斯巴基亞洛。 |
| They had two sons. | 他們有兩個兒子。 |
| The elder was One Batz, and the younger was One Chouen. | 哥哥叫巴茲，弟弟叫丘文。 |
| The two brothers passed all their gifts on to the boys. | 兩兄弟把自己的才能都傳給了孩子。 |
| One Batz and One Chouen grew up to be flute players and singers, writers and carvers, jade cutters and workers of gold and silver. | 巴茲與丘文長大後，成了吹笛者與歌者、書寫者與雕刻者、琢玉匠與金銀匠。 |
| Grandmother watched her grandsons grow. | 祖母看著孫子長大。 |
| But in time, Xbaquiyalo passed away. | 只是後來，斯巴基亞洛過世了。 |

**與繁中的差異**：繁中「會吹笛、會唱歌……」是動詞，英文改成三組成對的職業名詞，保留原典「三組對句」的結構（p.100 註 230）；用字是改寫，沒有照 Christenson 的「flautists and singers, writers and sculptors, jade workers and precious metalsmiths」。

## 6. The Gear and the Game（球具與球賽）

**插圖**：`book_front_06`

> What One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu loved most was throwing dice and playing ball, and they played every day. The four of them split into two teams and played against each other.
>
> They had a fine set of gear: leather pads, a yoke for the hips, arm guards, headdresses, masks, and a rubber ball.
>
> Whenever they played in the ballcourt, a falcon flew down to watch. He was the messenger of Heart of Sky, and in the blink of an eye he could fly back to the sky.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| What One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu loved most was throwing dice and playing ball, | 一胡納普和七胡納普最喜歡的，就是擲骰子和打球， |
| and they played every day. | 天天都玩。 |
| The four of them split into two teams and played against each other. | 他們四個人分成兩隊，彼此對打。 |
| They had a fine set of gear: | 他們有一整套漂亮的球具： |
| leather pads, a yoke for the hips, arm guards, headdresses, masks, and a rubber ball. | 皮護具、護腰（套在腰上的軛）、護臂、頭飾、面具，還有一顆橡膠球。 |
| Whenever they played in the ballcourt, a falcon flew down to watch. | 每當他們在球場上打球，就有一隻隼飛下來看。 |
| He was the messenger of Heart of Sky, | 牠是天心的使者， |
| and in the blink of an eye he could fly back to the sky. | 一眨眼就能飛回天上。 |

**與繁中的差異**：「一家四個人」寫成「The four of them」，前一張已交代是父子四人；「護腰」用原典的 yoke（註 244），加「for the hips」讓英文讀者知道是什麼。隼不寫名字 Wak（名稱表 A06）。

## 7. The Noise Reaches Xibalba（球聲驚動西巴爾巴）

**插圖**：`book_front_07`

> That ballcourt lay on the road to Xibalba. The pounding of feet and the shouting carried all the way down into the underworld.
>
> One Death and Seven Death looked up. "What is all that noise up on the earth? They jump and shout over our heads and show us no respect at all! Call them down here to play ball, and we will beat them."
>
> The lords gathered to make their plans. They had their eyes on that fine set of gear, too.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| That ballcourt lay on the road to Xibalba. | 那座球場，就在通往西巴爾巴的路上。 |
| The pounding of feet and the shouting carried all the way down into the underworld. | 腳步聲、叫喊聲，一路傳到了地底。 |
| One Death and Seven Death looked up. | 一死神與七死神抬起頭。 |
| "What is all that noise up on the earth? | 「地面上在吵什麼？ |
| They jump and shout over our heads and show us no respect at all! | 他們在我們頭上又跳又叫，一點也不敬重我們！ |
| Call them down here to play ball, and we will beat them." | 把他們叫下來打球，我們要打敗他們。」 |
| The lords gathered to make their plans. | 諸王聚在一起商量。 |
| They had their eyes on that fine set of gear, too. | 他們也看上了那一整套漂亮的球具。 |

**與繁中的差異**：繁中「打敗他們」沒有主詞，英文補上「we」。諸王的話是改寫：沒有照 Christenson 的「They are just stomping about and shouting」「They have neither honor nor respect」（p.101），只留「over our heads」這個短語。

## 8. The Game Above — End of Part One（地面上的球賽，前篇結尾）

**插圖**：`book_front_08`

> Up on the earth, the ball was still bouncing across the court. One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu set themselves once more and, laughing, struck the ball back.
>
> They noticed nothing at all.
>
> The sun had still not risen, and the earth still had no shadows. And far beneath their feet, the lords of the underworld had already settled on a plan.

| 英文 | 回譯 |
|---|---|
| Up on the earth, the ball was still bouncing across the court. | 地面上，球還在場上彈跳。 |
| One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu set themselves once more and, laughing, struck the ball back. | 一胡納普和七胡納普又一次擺好架勢，笑著把球打回去。 |
| They noticed nothing at all. | 他們什麼也沒有察覺。 |
| The sun had still not risen, and the earth still had no shadows. | 太陽依然沒有升起，大地依然沒有影子。 |
| And far beneath their feet, the lords of the underworld had already settled on a plan. | 而在他們腳下深處，地底的諸王已經想好了主意。 |

**與繁中的差異**：「腳下」寫成「far beneath their feet」（腳下深處），強調距離。
