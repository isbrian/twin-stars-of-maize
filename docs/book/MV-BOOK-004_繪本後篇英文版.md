## 2. The Fathers' Descent（父輩下行）

**插圖**：`book_back_02`

> The brothers followed the owls down a flight of steep steps.
>
> They went through Trembling Canyon and Murmuring Canyon. They crossed Scorpion River and were not stung. They crossed Blood River safely, since they never drank its water. Then they crossed Pus River.
>
> At last they came to a crossroads: a red road, a black road, a white road, and a yellow road. The black road spoke up: "Come my way! I am the road of the lords."
>
> Right there, the brothers were fooled, and they set off down the black road.

| One became the sun; the other, the moon. | 一個成了太陽，一個成了月亮。 |
| The Four Hundred Boys became stars as well. | 四百少年也成了星星。 |
| In the game: After the farewell, the player guides the twins all the way up to the sky. | 遊戲裡：告別後，玩家操作雙胞胎一路往上到天空。 |
| When the first true light shines down, shadows appear at last. | 第一道真光照下，影子才終於出現。 |

**與繁中的差異**：「你的名字不會被遺忘」寫成「No one will forget your name」，避開 Christenson 的原句「Your name shall not be forgotten」；「父親的名字」寫成複數 fathers'（一胡納普、七胡納普），告別時的 father 是單數，照原典 p.176～177 只對一胡納普說話。
